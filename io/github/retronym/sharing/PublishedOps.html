PublishedOps

io.github.retronym.sharing.PublishedOps
trait PublishedOps

The operations on a Published field, inherited by its companion, so found with no import.

Defined outside the opaque types' scope: an inline method inside it leaves the inliner's proxy for the scope, a dead load of Published's module, at every call site. The macros see through Published[T] and Handle where they need to (each erases to what it is over).
